About the project

Why this site exists

One place to search

Finding a badminton tournament in the United States usually means checking TournamentSoftware, then a few club Facebook pages, then a college club's Instagram, and then piecing the dates together yourself. This site does that legwork in one place. Every tournament and league you can still sign up for sits in one list, sorted so the deadline closing soonest is at the top.

Events that are easy to miss

It also makes room for the events that never reach TournamentSoftware. A lot of good tournaments get announced in nothing but a Facebook post or a college club's Instagram, so unless you already follow that club, you never hear about them. Those events are tracked here next to the sanctioned ones, with a link back to the original post so you can confirm the details for yourself.

About this badminton tournament list

This page tracks badminton tournaments and leagues held across the United States that are still open for registration. It covers USA Badminton (USAB) sanctioned events such as Open Local Championships, Open Regional Championships and the U.S. National Championships, plus BWF-sanctioned international events held on US soil, independent club opens, community tournaments, and college badminton club tournaments.

Listings are gathered from TournamentSoftware, usabadminton.org, club websites, Facebook event pages, and college club Instagram accounts, then sorted so the soonest registration deadline appears first. You can also sort badminton tournaments near you by distance from any US address, by prize money for events that offer a cash purse, or by how many players have entered so far. Entry counts come from TournamentSoftware and are refreshed daily, so treat them as a good indication of turnout rather than a live figure.
